Common Questions

We offer a complete continuum from Early Years (ages 3–5) through Primary School (Grades 1–5) to Secondary School (Grades 6–12). Our curriculum is designed as a seamless journey where each stage builds intentionally on the previous one.

The admissions process begins with an online inquiry form on our Admissions page. After submitting an inquiry, our team will contact you to schedule a campus tour and guide you through the assessment and enrollment steps. Applications for the following academic year typically open in January.

Required documents include: student identification (birth certificate or passport), academic records from the previous two years, current immunization and health forms, and proof of residence. Additional documents may be requested based on grade level. See our Admissions page for a complete checklist.

Yes, we offer 40+ student-led clubs and activities including athletics (15+ varsity teams), visual and performing arts, robotics, debate, Model UN, environmental action, coding, journalism, and service-learning programs. All students are encouraged to participate in at least one extracurricular activity each semester.

Parents can reach us by phone at +1 (234) 567-890, email at info@suffaeducation.edu, or through the contact form on our Contact page. Our office hours are Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM. For urgent matters outside office hours, an emergency contact line is available.

Our average student-to-teacher ratio is 12:1, with Early Years classes maintaining a 8:1 ratio. This ensures personalized attention and allows our educators to truly know each student's strengths, challenges, and learning style.

Yes, we offer need-based financial aid to qualified families. Approximately 25% of our student body receives some form of assistance. Applications for financial aid are processed separately from admissions and are kept strictly confidential. Contact our admissions office for application deadlines and requirements.

Our campus features 24/7 surveillance, secured entry points with visitor management, on-site security personnel, and a fully staffed medical center with registered nurses. We conduct regular safety drills, maintain comprehensive emergency protocols, and partner with local law enforcement and healthcare providers for rapid response.
